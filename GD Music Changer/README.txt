Hello! So to use my GD Background Music Changer, you just have to make sure the song you want to use is in Downloads on your PC.
To actually open the app, you have to double click on the "dist" folder and then double click on "GD Music Changer.exe".
Then, type your .mp3 file name that should be placed in Downloads in the text slot and press the "Turn into Background Music" button! In the text slot, you need to include .mp3 at the end.
Also, your old background music gets a backup save. Anyways have fun with your new background music!
